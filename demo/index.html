We tried to stop a small AI from forgetting English. It only half worked.

Teach Llama-3.2-1B Hindi and its English can suffer. Our idea, BhashaJEPA, asks the model to keep its Hindi close to its own English understanding. We tested it against the standard fixes, and we wrote our success rules before the first run.

Differences under about 4 points are within test noise, so the first two rows mean “about none”. BhashaJEPA under full fine-tuning lost 13.9 points (single run), about the same as plain fine-tuning, not less.

The problem

Fine-tuning an English instruct model on a new language can damage the English it already had. The usual fix is replaying English data, but that needs old training data or English answers, which teams often don’t have.

The idea

A Hindi instruction and its English original mean the same thing. While the model learns Hindi, we also ask it to predict how the original model understood the English version. Only translated instructions are needed, never English answers.

loss = Hindi language loss + λ · (1 − cosine( predicted meaning of the Hindi instruction, frozen English meaning ))

What we found

  • Hindi was learned: loss fell from 1.43 to about 1.14 for every method.
  • BhashaJEPA moved English internals less than naive fine-tuning: about 13% lower layer drift (one seed).
  • It did not measurably reduce forgetting. With LoRA there was almost nothing to reduce, and under full fine-tuning it lost about as much English as the naive baseline.
  • Belebele reading scores stayed near chance at this scale, so they can’t show Hindi or Kannada gains. No transfer to Kannada was observed.

HypothesisStatus

H4: drift explains forgettingPartly: lower drift, but no forgetting to link it to

Limits we want you to know about

  • One small model (1B), mostly LoRA, 3,000 machine-translated Hindi examples.
  • Only B1 and J1 have three seeds. Everything else is a single run.
  • A 1B model’s Hindi is still weak, and its Kannada is not usable.
  • We did not test safety behaviour after multilingual tuning.
